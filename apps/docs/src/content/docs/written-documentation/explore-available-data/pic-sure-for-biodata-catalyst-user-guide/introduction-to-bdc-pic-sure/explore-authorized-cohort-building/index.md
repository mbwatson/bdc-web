# Explore: Authorized Cohort Building

Explore allows users with an eRA Commons account to search variables, apply filters, build cohorts, and export data from studies they are authorized to access.

***

## Requirements

To obtain access to Explore, you must have an NIH eRA Commons account. For instructions on how to register an account, please refer to the [eRA website](https://www.era.nih.gov/register-accounts/register-in-era-commons.htm).

## Login

After you have created an eRA Commons account, you can log in to Explore by navigating to [https://picsure.biodatacatalyst.nhlbi.nih.gov/login](https://picsure.biodatacatalyst.nhlbi.nih.gov/login) and selecting to log in **with eRA Commons**. You will be directed to the NIH website to log in with your eRA Commons credentials. After signing in and accepting the terms of the agreement on the NIH RAS Information Sharing Consent page, allow the _BDC Powered by Gen3 (BDC-Gen3)_ service to manage your authorization.
